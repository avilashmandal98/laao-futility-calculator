What is driving this estimate (marginal change vs a typical/median patient; approximate, not exact Shapley)
Research use only — not a validated clinical decision tool. Derived from retrospective de-identified data; not prospectively validated. Absolute percentages were calibrated to the development population; the optional recalibration corrects the average level (calibration-in-the-large) but not the calibration slope — a full recalibration needs your site's own paired predictions and outcomes. Prefer the risk band / relative stratification over the exact percentage. Does not account for procedural details, device type, frailty, or goals of care. High predicted mortality flags competing risk for shared decision-making; it is not an exclusion rule.
